# Benchmarks

heifer 0.3.28 against the other HEIC decoders and encoders, measured in November 2026, on an Apple M5 (4 performance + 6 efficiency cores), macOS 27.0.1. Reproduce with `python3 benchmarks/run.py` (macOS, ffmpeg with libx265, numpy).

Competitors: `heic-rs` 0.1.4, `heic` 0.1.6, `heic-decoder` 0.1.1, `zune-heic` 0.5.0, `still265` 0.6.0, ffmpeg 9.0.1, and Apple's ImageIO (`sips`, hardware HEVC).

Test files: the 9 Portrait backgrounds of macOS (Apple photos, 1920×1080), a 12 MP image encoded by Apple and by heifer, an image with transparency and a 10-bit gradient (generated by the script).

## Encoding

All encoders produce 4:2:0, except `still265-slow-444` (4:4:4, which hardware decoders of phones usually do not support). BD-rate: average size difference at equal quality, against heifer (negative: smaller files than heifer). Quality is the PSNR of the decoded image against the source, decoded by heifer (which reads every file correctly) and by Apple (what Apple's apps show).

### photo

| Encoder | BD-rate (decoded by heifer) | BD-rate (decoded by Apple) | Time per file | 1 thread |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `heifer` | reference | reference | 425 ms | 1225 ms |
| `still265-fast` | +14.2% | wrong colours | 216 ms | same |
| `still265-slow` | +0.1% | wrong colours | 432 ms | same |
| `still265-slow-444` | -6.8% | wrong colours | 526 ms | same |
| `apple` (hardware) | +51.0% | +50.5% | 89 ms | — |

## Against libheif

Decoding the four real photos of `tests/fixtures/real`: time of the whole command, PNG output included, median of 5 runs. heifer uses the PNG encoder of the `image` crate; libheif is given `--png-compression-level 1`, close to its speed (its default level is about four times slower here).
