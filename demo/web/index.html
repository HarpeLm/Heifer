What's inside

HEVC stills & sequences

A from-scratch H.265 decoder with I, P and hierarchical B-frames, TMVP, 8–12 bit and 4:0:0–4:4:4.

Parallel grid tiles

iPhone photos are grids of up to 48 tiles, Samsung's 200 MP files have 768. Natively, they are decoded in parallel.

HEIC & HEICS encoding

From-scratch HEVC intra & inter encoder with rate-distortion decisions, alpha, GIF/APNG sequences and 512×512 tiles.

EXIF / XMP / ICC

Metadata is read without decoding pixels. Rotation, crop and mirror are applied for you.

No unsafe Rust

#![forbid(unsafe_code)], size limits, and continuous fuzzing on CI.

Use it in Rust

# Cargo.toml
heifer = "0.7"

let bytes = std::fs::read("photo.heic")?;
let image = heifer::decode(&bytes)?;   // rotation, crop and alpha applied
let rgba = image.to_rgba8();

let heic = heifer::encode(&image, &heifer::EncodeOptions::default())?;
std::fs::write("copy.heic", heic)?;
